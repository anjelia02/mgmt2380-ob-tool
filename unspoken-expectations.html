Agree on expectations before they cause problems

Many working relationships depend on expectations nobody has said out loud, such as how fast to reply or how often to get feedback. This tool helps two people bring those expectations into the open and agree on a few of them.

Your answers stay in this browser. You share only a code with your partner, and only when you choose to.

How it works. Each of you answers alone. You rate what you expect, then guess what your partner expects. Results appear only after you have both shared your codes.

Sort your expectations

For each statement, set two levels from 1 (not expected) to 5 (strongly expected).

Why guess their side? Many surprises come from wrong guesses about what the other person expects.

Share your code, then get theirs

Your answers are now locked. Copy your code and send it to your partner by a channel you trust. Paste their code below to unlock the reveal.
