Agree on what each of you owes the other

Many working relationships rest on promises nobody has stated out loud, such as how fast to reply or how often to get feedback. This tool helps two people see those promises the same way and agree on a few of them.

Your answers stay in this browser. You share only a code with your partner, and only when you choose to.

How it works. Each of you answers alone. For each item you say whether it was promised or agreed, how much each of you owes the other, and what you think your partner believes they owe you. Results appear only after you have both shared your codes.

Sort what is owed

For each item, answer four questions. Use the scale from 1 (not owed) to 5 (clearly owed).

Owed is not the same as wanted. You may want fast replies without believing your partner promised them. Answer what you believe, not what you would like.

Share your code, then get theirs

Your answers are now locked. Copy your code and send it to your partner by a channel you trust. Paste their code below to unlock the reveal.
